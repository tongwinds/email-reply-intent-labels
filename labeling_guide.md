# Labeling Guide: Outbound Email Replies (Q3 2026)

**Source:** `Q3_2026_Outbound_Email_Reply_Export.csv` (840 replies, exported by Olivia Grant)
**Goal:** Label every reply by buying intent so we can check which signals predict a paid upgrade.
**Labels:** `High`, `Medium`, `Low`, `None`, plus `Auto-reply` for automatic responses (use exactly these five values, one per reply)

## How to label

Read `reply_body` and ask: **what is this person telling us about buying Notion?**

1. Is there a request that only a buyer would make (a quote, an order form, a seat count, a contract)? Label **High**.
2. Are they asking real questions about the product or comparing it with another tool? Label **Medium**.
3. Did they answer, but with no real interest or no ability to act? Label **Low**.
4. Did they clearly reject us? Label **None**.
5. Is it an automatic message with no human answer yet (out of office, on leave, unmonitored mailbox)? Label **Auto-reply**.

If a reply fits two labels, use the **higher** label only when the reply contains a concrete buying detail (a price request, a seat count, a deadline). Otherwise use the lower one.

---

## High: Ready to buy now

**Meaning:** The person is asking for pricing, a quote, a contract, an order form, or a plan upgrade, usually with a seat count or a timeline. A rep should call these first.

**Examples**

| Reply ID | Reply |
|---|---|
| RPL-0244 | "Please send an order form for Business, roughly 140 seats. We'd like to start next month." |
| RPL-0191 | "Our finance lead needs a quote for 17 seats before month end. Who do I talk to?" |
| RPL-0277 | "We have 23 people in the workspace already. What's the process to upgrade everyone and get SSO?" |
| RPL-0176 | "Is there a volume discount above 22 seats? We're comparing your quote to Confluence's." |

**Also High:** "We're ready to move the team over. What does Enterprise cost for 49 users, and can you send a contract?" (RPL-0024)

---

## Medium: Interested, needs more information

**Meaning:** The person is evaluating Notion. They ask product, security, or migration questions, or they are comparing us with another tool. There is no quote request yet, so the next step is a helpful answer, not a close.

**Examples**

| Reply ID | Reply |
|---|---|
| RPL-0056 | "Does Business include SAML SSO and audit logs, or is that Enterprise only?" |
| RPL-0011 | "Can Notion sync with Jira and Slack? That would decide it for us." |
| RPL-0017 | "We're trialling a few tools for our 41-person team. What makes Notion better for wikis?" |
| RPL-0022 | "Forwarding to our head of engineering, who owns tooling decisions." |

**Also Medium:** data residency for EU customers (RPL-0039), how permissions work across teamspaces (RPL-0015), importing Confluence spaces with page history (RPL-0027).

---

## Low: Responds, but no real interest

**Meaning:** The person replied politely or pointed us elsewhere, but there is no active interest and no ability to buy. This includes soft "not now" answers, "check back later" answers, and people who say we have the wrong contact. Students asking about discounts for themselves also land here, because they are not a team buying a paid plan.

**Examples**

| Reply ID | Reply |
|---|---|
| RPL-0001 | "Not this quarter. The free plan works for us for now." |
| RPL-0021 | "Interesting, but budgets are frozen until next year. Check back in Q1?" |
| RPL-0012 | "I'm not the right person. Our IT lead handles software purchases." |
| RPL-0215 | "Is there a student discount? A few of us in my class use Notion and want Plus for cheaper." |

**Also Low:** "Love the product, happy on free for the moment." (RPL-0019), "I just use it for my own notes. You'd want to talk to our ops manager." (RPL-0090), "Wrong person, sorry. Procurement decides on anything with more than 131 users." (RPL-0004)

---

## None: Not interested

**Meaning:** A direct rejection, an unsubscribe request, or a statement that they already chose another tool. Reps should not follow up.

**Examples**

| Reply ID | Reply |
|---|---|
| RPL-0079 | "Unsubscribe." |
| RPL-0043 | "We don't plan to pay for a docs tool. Please stop emailing." |
| RPL-0016 | "We just signed with Confluence for the whole company, so we're all set." |

**Also None:** "Not interested, thanks." (RPL-0002), "Please remove me from this list." (RPL-0008), "We're consolidating on Microsoft Loop, so no need to follow up." (RPL-0007)

---

## Auto-reply: Automatic message, no human answer yet

**Meaning:** The reply was generated by an email system, not written by a person. It says nothing about interest, so it should not count as High, Medium, Low or None. These accounts have not answered yet, and a rep can try again later.

**Examples**

| Reply ID | Reply |
|---|---|
| RPL-0006 | "I'm out of the office with limited email access and will reply when I'm back." |
| RPL-0116 | "This mailbox is not monitored. For urgent matters contact the front desk." |

**Also Auto-reply:** "I'm travelling this week and will get back to you as soon as I can." and "Thanks for your message. I'm on leave until the 2nd and will respond then."

**Note:** If the person writes back after returning, that new reply gets its own label. "Mailbox not monitored" will never produce a human answer, so a rep may want a different contact for that account.
---

## Edge cases and decisions

| Situation | Label | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Out of office, on leave, travelling, or "mailbox not monitored" | Auto-reply | Automatic message, so there is no buying signal and no rejection. Keep it separate from Low and None. |
| "Wrong person" or "talk to our IT lead / ops manager" with no interest shown | Low | We got a response but the contact cannot act. The account may still be worth a new contact. |
| "Forwarding to the person who owns tooling decisions" | Medium | Passing us to the decision maker is a positive step. |
| Student or study group asking about price, discount, or Education plan | Low | No budget or purchase authority. Matches the call board rule that discounts student accounts. |
| "Check back in Q1" or "revisit after planning cycle" | Low | Polite delay, no concrete need today. |
| Already signed with Confluence or moved to Microsoft Loop | None | The decision is made. |
| Comparing Notion with Confluence but asking only product questions | Medium | Evaluating, not asking for a quote. |
| Comparing quotes ("comparing your quote to Confluence's") | High | They are asking for pricing at a specific size. |
| Seat count mentioned but no pricing or upgrade request ("trialling tools for our 41-person team") | Medium | A big team is a good sign, but the reply is still asking for information. |

## Notes for whoever labels these

- Label the **reply text only**. Do not use `domain_type`, campaign, or sender to change the label. The one exception is the student rule above, which depends on what the reply says.
- Seat numbers vary from reply to reply, but the wording repeats. Many replies are identical or near identical, so apply the same label to the same wording.
- Output one label per `reply_id` and keep the values exactly `High`, `Medium`, `Low`, `None`, or `Auto-reply`.

## Expected spread (from a read-through of the export)

A first pass with these rules gives about: **High 66, Medium 221, Low 310, None 174, Auto-reply 69** (840 total). When measuring which signals predict a paid upgrade, leave the Auto-reply group out, since it carries no intent. If a model's labels are far from this, check the edge cases above first.
